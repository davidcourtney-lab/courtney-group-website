Viruses carry very few genes of their own, so they rely on the host cell's RNA-binding proteins to process, move and translate their RNA.

In this paper we developed an approach to identify the cellular RNA-binding proteins that interact with influenza RNA during infection. Among them, the protein **MKRN2** turned out to play a role in trafficking influenza mRNA.

**Read the paper:** Bonazza S, Coutts HL, Sukumar S, Turkington HL, Courtney DG. *PLoS Pathogens* 20(5): e1012231 (2024).
