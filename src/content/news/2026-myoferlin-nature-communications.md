Influenza A virus packages its eight genome segments as viral ribonucleoproteins (vRNPs). After they leave the nucleus, those vRNPs have to travel through the cytoplasm to the plasma membrane, where new virus particles bud.

In this study we identified **Myoferlin** as a component of the late-stage trafficking vesicles that carry vRNPs on that journey, and found a role for it with other enveloped RNA viruses as well.

Congratulations to everyone in the group and to our collaborators at the Wellcome-Wolfson Institute for Experimental Medicine.

**Read the paper:** Bonazza S, Turkington HL, Sukumar S, Peate E, Coutts HL, Montgomery JJ, Hawthorn C, Getty EMPE, Touzelet O, Barabas J, Power UF, Courtney DG. *Nature Communications* 17 (2026). [doi:10.1038/s41467-026-69386-0](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-69386-0)
