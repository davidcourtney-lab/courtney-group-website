Where a viral RNA is inside the cell decides what happens to it: whether it is translated, replicated, packaged or recognised by the immune system.

Our review in *PLoS Pathogens* brings together what is known about influenza A virus RNA localisation and the host trafficking pathways that move those RNAs between the nucleus, the cytoplasm and the plasma membrane.

**Read the review:** Bonazza S, Courtney DG. Influenza A virus RNA localisation and the interceding trafficking pathways of the host cell. *PLoS Pathogens* 21(4): e1013090 (2025).
