Just in time for Christmas, **Dr Hannah Turkington** has been awarded a **BBSRC Postdoctoral Fellowship**.

Influenza A viruses circulate in birds and other animals, and these zoonotic reservoirs are where new pandemic strains can come from. Hannah's fellowship will look at how the virus's genome, packaged as viral ribonucleoproteins (vRNPs), is trafficked through cells from these important reservoir hosts.

This is a big achievement and a well-deserved one. A huge congratulations to Hannah, and a very Merry Christmas from the whole lab! 🎄
