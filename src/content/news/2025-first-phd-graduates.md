On 26 June 2025, **Dr Stefano Bonazza** and **Dr Hannah Coutts**, the first two PhD students to join the lab, graduated from Queen's University Belfast.

Both are authors on our papers on MKRN2 and influenza mRNA trafficking (*PLoS Pathogens*, 2024) and on Myoferlin and late-stage vRNP trafficking (*Nature Communications*, 2026).

Congratulations, Drs Bonazza and Coutts. We are very proud of you.
