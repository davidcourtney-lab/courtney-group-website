This study began during David's Marie Skłodowska-Curie fellowship at Institut Pasteur and was completed as a co-led project between our two groups.

By mapping the RNA-binding proteins bound to influenza A virus mRNA (its "RBPome"), the team identified **TDP-43** as a host protein involved in viral replication.

**Read the paper:** Dupont M, Krischuns T, et al., Courtney DG, Naffakh N. *Nucleic Acids Research* (2024). [doi:10.1093/nar/gkae291](https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkae291)
