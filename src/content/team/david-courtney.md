David has been fascinated by how viruses replicate and manipulate their hosts since early in his career. After a degree in Biomedical Sciences with a year at GlaxoSmithKline and a PhD in molecular biology at Ulster University, he held postdoctoral posts at Duke University and Institut Pasteur, supported by a Marie Skłodowska-Curie Global Fellowship. He set up The Courtney Group at Queen's University Belfast in 2021 with an ERC Starting Grant, and was promoted to Senior Lecturer in 2025. He is a STEM NI Ambassador and a Fellow of the Winston Churchill Memorial Trust.
